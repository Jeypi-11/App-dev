# Favorite Movie: Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)

**Avengers: Age of Ultron** is an epic superhero action-adventure film produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Joss Whedon.

## Overview
When Tony Stark and Bruce Banner try to jump-start a dormant peacekeeping program called **Ultron**, their creations go terribly awry. The villainous artificial intelligence decides that saving Earth requires destroying humanity. Earth's Mightiest Heroes—Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, the Incredible Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye—must reassemble to defeat Ultron and stop his global threat.

## Key Highlights & Characters
* **Tony Stark / Iron Man** – Co-creator of Ultron, driven by a vision to build armor around the world.
* **Ultron** – A menacing rogue AI voiced by James Spader who vows human extinction.
* **Wanda & Pietro Maximoff** – Powerful newcomers (Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver) who initially join Ultron before realizing his true intentions.
* **Vision** – An advanced synthetic android born from the Mind Stone and Jarvis's core programming.
* **High-Octane Battles** – Features iconic set pieces including the Hulkbuster fight in Johannesburg and the final battle in Sokovia.
